Questions

What sizes does App Store Connect need?

A 6.9″ iPhone set (1320 × 2868) and a 13″ iPad set (2064 × 2752) are enough for most apps, because App Store Connect scales them down for smaller devices. You can upload up to 10 screenshots per size.

What size are Google Play screenshots?

16:9 or 9:16, commonly 1080 × 1920 for phones, 1200 × 1920 for 7″ tablets and 1600 × 2560 for 10″ tablets. Google Play takes up to 8 per device type.

Can I make landscape screenshots for games?

Yes. Tap Portrait above the preview to switch to Landscape. Every export size is rotated.

Are my screenshots private?

Yes. Everything is drawn in your browser. Your project is saved only on this device so you can come back to it.
